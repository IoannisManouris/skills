# Visual references: inspiration is not texture permission

## Access protocol

Open the user's actual reference first. The following is a curated starting index, not a complete style atlas and not evidence that every video was watched. URLs were identified from primary creator pages; video playback was not available during this build. Do not infer timing from a title/thumbnail. For Unity/Unreal examples, study design and motion; translate to actual Roblox primitives, and disclose approximations for unsupported shaders. Assets offered under a video may have separate paid/custom terms.

## Fill style-specific gaps at task time

This index is weighted toward accessible real-time demonstrations and basic art libraries; it is **not** an evenly sampled collection of every style. For painterly, realistic, paper-cut, horror, pixel or other requests, find a relevant original creator's work or use the user's supplied reference and record the link. Search the family plus the specific effect, then inspect actual frames and source rights. Do not let readily available anime tutorials dictate a non-anime brief.

Save a per-task reference note: URL/local file; creator; access status; observed shape/palette; observed timing or unknown; desired transferable traits; traits not to copy; and rights category. This skill does not redistribute video frames, thumbnails or third-party reference images.
